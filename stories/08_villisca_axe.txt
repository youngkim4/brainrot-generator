In 1912, someone entered a house in Iowa with the family's own axe and killed eight people in their beds. Six of them were children. The killer then covered every mirror in the house, left a slab of raw bacon on the floor, and disappeared.

The Moore family came home from a church event that evening with their four children and two young sleepover guests, Ina and Lena Stillinger. They locked the doors. They went to sleep. They had no idea someone was already inside, waiting in the attic.

Cigarette butts were later found in the attic crawl space. The killer had been up there for hours, smoking in the dark, listening to the family settle into bed.

The murders started in the master bedroom. Josiah Moore was struck so many times with the blunt side of the axe that his face was unrecognizable. His wife Sarah was killed beside him. She never woke up.

Then the killer went room to room. Herman, eleven. Katherine, ten. Boyd, seven. Paul, five. Ina, eight. Lena, twelve. One blow each to the head while they slept. Eight people murdered in complete silence. Not a single scream.

When the bodies were found the next morning, the crime scene was methodical. Every mirror in the house was draped with cloth. A bowl of bloody water sat on the kitchen table. The killer had washed their hands before leaving. A two-pound slab of raw bacon lay on the floor of the downstairs bedroom. No one has ever explained why.

The axe belonged to Josiah Moore. The killer brought nothing and took nothing. They used what was already in the house.

A traveling preacher named Reverend George Kelly confessed, then took it back, then was acquitted at trial. Historians have since linked the Villisca murders to a string of nearly identical axe killings across the Midwest between 1911 and 1912. All targeted families. All happened near railroad lines. All unsolved.

The house still stands in Villisca, Iowa. You can book a night there. Most people don't make it until morning.

Whoever waited in that attic was never caught. And between 1911 and 1912, they may have done this more than once.
